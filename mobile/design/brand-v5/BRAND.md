# FindEZ brand kit v5 (aurora theme)

The Petals mark in ink, inside an aurora-gradient theme: soft grainy pastel fields behind a black and white interface. This replaces the orange mark in kit v4. The petal shapes are unchanged.

## Logo files (`logo/`)

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `findez-mark.svg/.png` | Ink mark (#111112) on light or aurora backgrounds |
| `findez-mark-white.svg/.png` | White mark on dark backgrounds |
| `findez-mark-small.svg`, `-small-white.svg` | Wider gaps for sizes below 32px |
| `findez-wordmark.svg/.png` | Mark plus "FindEZ" in ink |
| `findez-wordmark-white.svg/.png` | Mark plus "FindEZ" in white |
| `findez-app-icon.svg/.png` (1024), `-180`, `-120` | **Main iOS app icon:** black tile, white petals, a faint peach and blue glow |
| `findez-app-icon-light.*` | Light app icon: white tile, ink petals |
| `findez-favicon.svg/.png` (512), `-32`, `-16` | Dark favicon |
| `findez-favicon-light.*` | Light favicon |
| `jpeg/` | JPEG copies of the marks, wordmarks and app icons |

## Colour

- **Ink #111112 and white** for the logo and the interface.
- **The aurora** is used only for backgrounds and picture panels, never inside the mark. Its colours: peach #E9B996, dusty blue #9FB4CF, lilac #C9B5D4 and sage #B3C8AF, with film grain.
- The app icon glow uses #D9906A and #6F89B0 at 60%, over #0E0E10.

## Rules

- Do not fill the mark with gradients or the aurora colours, and do not add effects to it.
- Leave clear space of at least a quarter of the mark's width.
- Minimum sizes: 16px for the favicon (small cut), 20px for the mark, 96px wide for the wordmark.
