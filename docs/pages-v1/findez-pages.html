Building FIND

Research at AI Robots Inc. on how machines understand the physical world.

Partner with us

FIND is AI Robots Inc.’s proprietary AI pipeline for understanding the physical world. It starts with visual perception inside FindEZ and works toward robots that learn new tasks and adapt to unfamiliar environments with less task-specific training.

Perception first

Today FIND powers Auto Extract. It finds the objects in a photo, reads their labels and barcodes, matches known parts and counts them.

What it reads

Each detection becomes an item draft with a confidence score. Low scores go to a person to confirm.

How FIND reads a photo

Simple checks run first. Larger models only step in when they are needed, which keeps answers fast and accurate.

Find objectsSeparate every item in the frame.
Read labelsBarcodes, part numbers and printed text.
Match partsCompare against known products and past photos.
