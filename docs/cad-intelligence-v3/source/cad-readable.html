How to use it.Export, upload, choose an inventory, compare.

Export from Fusion

Open your assembly and run Export FindEZ BOM from Utilities → Add-Ins.

Upload the BOM

Choose the findez-bom.json file, or a CSV or Excel parts list.

Select an inventory

Pick the organization and inventory you want to check against.

Compare

Review matches and quantities, then export the shortages as a CSV.

Supported files.Straight from Fusion, or a parts list you already have.

Files up to 5 MiB. Spreadsheets can have up to 5,000 rows, 200 columns and 20 sheets, and only the selected sheet is compared. Map Component name and Required quantity, and add identifiers if you have them. Download the CSV template

Matching accuracy.Part numbers and barcodes are stronger evidence than names alone.

An exact barcode, or a manufacturer plus part number, confirms a match. When only the name lines up, FindEZ marks the part as Needs review, so you can check it before buying or building.

Privacy.Component metadata goes to FindEZ. Your geometry stays in Fusion.

What’s uploadedChoosing Compare sends the parts metadata, like names, part numbers and quantities, to FindEZ for the inventory you select.
What stays in FusionThe add-in saves metadata to a local JSON file. It doesn’t upload your assembly geometry or ask for your password.

Current limitations.It tells you what you have. Decisions stay with you.

No automatic purchases

It shows what to buy and exports a shortages CSV. Ordering stays with you.

No stock reservations

Comparing doesn’t hold or check out any parts. Existing reservations do reduce what counts as available.

No design changes

Checking an assembly never edits the design or saves its parts into your inventory.

No guaranteed engineering compatibility

It doesn’t verify fit, electrical ratings or physical stock counts.
